## Short answer

Create a concise directory of the people and organisations involved in practical support, with their roles and preferred contact methods. A contact list is useful when it explains who can help with which task.

## Practical explanation

Confirm numbers directly and record when they were last checked. Include the clinic's normal booking contact and the agreed family coordinator. Ask before sharing someone's personal number with a wider group. Keep a copy accessible to the people who need it. Review the list after staff changes, moves or changes in caregiving responsibility. Use the provider's guidance for urgent situations rather than assuming a routine contact is always monitored.

## Example

A label such as “reception, appointment changes, weekday office hours” is more actionable than a saved number labelled only “clinic”.
