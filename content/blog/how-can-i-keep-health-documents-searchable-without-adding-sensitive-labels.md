## Short answer

Use descriptive document types, dates and provider names, with personal records grouped in private folders. Searchability does not require putting detailed diagnoses or identifiers into filenames that may appear in previews or shared lists.

## Practical explanation

Choose a limited vocabulary such as “visit summary”, “report” and “invoice”. Apply it consistently when saving new documents. Keep more sensitive context inside the protected record where it belongs. Search for a sample document to check that the naming pattern works. If a provider supplies an official filename with an identifier, preserve the original in a controlled location when making a simplified working copy.

## Example

Searching “2026-11 visit summary” can retrieve a document without exposing its clinical contents in the filename.
