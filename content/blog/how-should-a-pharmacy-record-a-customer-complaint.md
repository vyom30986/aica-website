## Short answer

Record the customer's concern accurately, the date, relevant transaction or enquiry reference and the person handling the response. Separate the customer's account from staff findings and agreed actions.

## Practical explanation

Acknowledge the complaint through the pharmacy's normal process. Preserve relevant records and route clinical concerns to the appropriate professional. Agree on the next communication step and record it. Avoid promising an outcome before the facts have been reviewed. When the matter is resolved, document the action and the customer's response if provided. Use access controls appropriate to the information involved.

## Example

A complaint entry can distinguish “customer reported a delayed callback” from “review confirmed the callback task had no assigned owner”.
