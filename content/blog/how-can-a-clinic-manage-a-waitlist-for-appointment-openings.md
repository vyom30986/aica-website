## Short answer

Keep a structured list of requests with the relevant service, contact preference, availability and the clinic's agreed prioritisation process. Record offers and responses so staff can see which slots remain available.

## Practical explanation

Confirm that the patient wants to be contacted about openings. Define who reviews suitability and priority when clinical judgement is involved. When offering a slot, record the time, response deadline if applicable and outcome. Avoid offering one confirmed slot to multiple people without a clear process. Remove or update requests when patients no longer need them. Review the list for outdated details.

## Example

A waitlist record should show whether a patient was offered a slot and declined, could not be reached or is still considering the offer.
