## Short answer

Ask which tasks they want help with and let them practise those tasks on their own device. Support works best when the person remains involved in decisions about their records, reminders and communications.

## Practical explanation

Begin with one common task, such as opening an appointment detail. Explain each step in familiar language and give the person time to repeat it. Adjust text size or accessibility settings according to their preference. Agree before changing account or notification settings. Write down a short guide if helpful, and review which actions should always be confirmed with them.

## Example

Instead of setting up every feature at once, practise opening a report and returning to the home screen until the person feels comfortable doing it independently.
