## Short answer

Review open requests by age, owner and next action, then identify the specific dependency preventing progress. A useful review produces a decision or assignment for each stalled item.

## Practical explanation

Separate requests waiting for customers, suppliers and internal review. Check whether promised updates were sent. Reassign tasks when the original owner is unavailable and record the handover. Look for repeated causes such as missing branch details or unclear status definitions. Keep the review focused on resolving the request and improving the process, with sensitive information visible only to the appropriate team.

## Example

Five old requests with no owner suggest an assignment problem. Five requests awaiting the same supplier suggest a different next action.
