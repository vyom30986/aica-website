## Short answer

Test valid submissions, required fields, optional fields, confirmation wording and the destination where staff receive requests. The test should show that a request is usable by reception, not merely that the page displays a success message.

## Practical explanation

Use synthetic details and test both desktop and mobile layouts. Check that the selected branch or service arrives in the correct field. Verify that failed submissions explain how to retry or contact the clinic. Look for duplicates after a retry and confirm that staff can identify the submission time. Check accessibility with keyboard navigation and readable labels. Record issues and repeat the affected tests after repair.

## Example

A form that saves the name but drops the selected branch creates extra work even though the technical submission succeeds.
