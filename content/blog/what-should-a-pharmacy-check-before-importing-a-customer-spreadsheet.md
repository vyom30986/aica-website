## Short answer

Review column meanings, duplicate records, missing identifiers and contact preferences before importing. A technically successful upload can still produce records that staff misunderstand or cannot use reliably.

## Practical explanation

Make a protected backup of the source. Map each source column to the destination field and test a small permitted sample. Check shared phone numbers, leading zeros and text formatting. Verify how the system handles blank values and duplicates. Confirm access permissions before transferring personal data. After import, compare sample records with the source and record any exceptions that need correction.

## Example

A phone column interpreted as a number may lose formatting. Treat contact identifiers as text and check them in the destination before relying on the import.
