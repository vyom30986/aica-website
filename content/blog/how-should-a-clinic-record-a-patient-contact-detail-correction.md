## Short answer

Verify the correction through the clinic's usual process, update the authoritative record and check the systems that depend on it. A changed phone number is only useful if future reminders and callbacks use the corrected detail.

## Practical explanation

Record the date and source of the update according to clinic procedure. Check whether the number is personal or shared and confirm the preferred recipient. Update connected appointment or communication tools where necessary. Avoid retaining an obsolete contact as the default. If an earlier message went to the wrong destination, follow the clinic's privacy and incident-handling process.

## Example

A new number entered in a reception note may not update the reminder system. Confirm the change in the actual contact field used for messages.
