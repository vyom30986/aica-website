## Short answer

Select the specific document requested, confirm the recipient and send it through the channel the clinic has agreed to use. Sharing the relevant file keeps unrelated family conversations and documents out of the exchange.

## Practical explanation

Open the document first to verify the patient name and page count. Ask whether the clinic prefers a PDF, original download or image. Include a brief message with the appointment reference when appropriate. Check that a shared link gives the intended recipient access and does not expose a broader folder. Keep a record of what you sent and when, especially if the clinic later asks for it again.

## Example

Send “Report for booking 214, two pages attached” with the report itself rather than forwarding a conversation containing several people's records.
