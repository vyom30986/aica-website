## Short answer

Update the booking status, record the source and time of the cancellation and stop reminders tied to that appointment. Follow the clinic's process for any further action, including requests to arrange another visit.

## Practical explanation

Verify the booking reference before changing it. Distinguish cancellation from a rescheduling request or a missed appointment. Confirm the change with the patient through the agreed channel. Check whether related tasks such as document collection remain relevant. Route questions about clinical timing to the appropriate professional. Keep the change history understandable for staff reviewing the record later.

## Example

A patient cancelling one appointment may still have another active booking. Update the specific record rather than assuming all future appointments should be removed.
