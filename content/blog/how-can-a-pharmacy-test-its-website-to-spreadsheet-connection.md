## Short answer

Submit a clearly labelled synthetic enquiry through the normal form and verify that exactly one complete row arrives in the intended sheet. Check the whole path, including the acknowledgement shown to the person submitting it.

## Practical explanation

Use test contact details that do not belong to an unsuspecting person. Confirm each field maps to the correct column and that the submission time is understandable. Test a supported optional field and a retry scenario. Ensure manually entered follow-up notes remain intact when new enquiries arrive. Record the test outcome and remove or clearly retain the test row according to the team's agreed process.

## Example

A connection is working when a valid submission reaches the sheet, staff can assign it and a second submission does not overwrite the first enquiry's notes.
