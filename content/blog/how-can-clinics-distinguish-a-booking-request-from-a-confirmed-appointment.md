## Short answer

Use separate statuses and patient-facing wording for requested, offered and confirmed appointments. A form submission should only promise the stage that the booking system has actually completed.

## Practical explanation

Define who can confirm a slot and what checks are required. Keep pending requests in a visible queue with an owner. When a slot is offered, record whether the patient has accepted it according to the clinic's process. Send the final confirmation through the agreed channel. Avoid placing tentative requests into reminders that imply attendance is already scheduled.

## Example

A website can acknowledge “request received” immediately, then send a separate confirmation once reception has agreed the appointment time.
