## Short answer

Use one enquiry queue with the customer's request, contact preference, time received, responsible staff member and current status. A shared queue makes it easier for the next shift to see what needs attention.

## Practical explanation

Define a few clear statuses such as new, awaiting information, awaiting staff review and resolved. Record only the information needed for the request. Keep clinical questions assigned to the appropriate pharmacist. Review unanswered items at agreed times during the day. When a request is resolved, record the outcome rather than simply deleting it from view. Match access to each staff member's responsibilities.

## Example

A question about opening hours can be answered by the service team, while a question about a prescription should be routed to the pharmacist with its context intact.
