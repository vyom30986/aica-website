## Short answer

Make sure the requested files can be opened using the device you will take, and keep an agreed alternative if connectivity is unreliable. The backup should contain the relevant documents in a format you can actually retrieve.

## Practical explanation

Open the files before leaving home. Check that cloud-only documents have downloaded when offline access is needed. Keep paper originals available when the clinic asks for them. Protect the device with its normal access controls and remove temporary copies from borrowed devices afterwards. Ask the clinic about file-size limits or accepted formats before sending a large bundle.

## Example

A PDF that opens on your home computer may still need downloading to your phone. Test the exact device you plan to bring.
