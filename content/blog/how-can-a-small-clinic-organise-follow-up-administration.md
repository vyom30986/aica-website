## Short answer

Use a shared task list that records the agreed follow-up action, responsible staff member, due date and current status. Administrative staff should work from the clinician's documented plan and the clinic's established processes.

## Practical explanation

Define which tasks reception can handle and which need clinical review. Record booking requests, document collection and callbacks separately when they have different owners. Review due tasks at a predictable time each day. Keep the latest contact preferences available to the staff making arrangements. When a task is completed, record the actual outcome so the next person can understand what happened.

## Example

A follow-up may require both a report and a review appointment. Tracking those as separate tasks makes it clear when only one is complete.
