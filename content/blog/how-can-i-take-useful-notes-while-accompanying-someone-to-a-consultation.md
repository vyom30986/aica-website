## Short answer

Ask the person whether they want you to take notes, then record questions, explanations and agreed next steps clearly. Use the clinician's written summary as the reference when checking details afterwards.

## Practical explanation

Write the visit date and provider at the top. Separate your observations from the clinician's statements. Ask for clarification when you cannot capture an instruction accurately. Avoid recording audio or video without the necessary agreement. After the visit, review the notes with the person and identify any unresolved questions. Keep the note with the visit documents so it can be checked later.

## Example

A useful closing note records who will arrange the next appointment, which documents are expected and where to ask questions about the plan.
