## Short answer

Return to the provider's latest confirmation and establish one current record. Conflicting messages often reflect different stages of a booking, so identify the source and time of each update before making new arrangements.

## Practical explanation

Ask the person coordinating the booking to contact the clinic if the conflict remains unresolved. Record the confirmed date, time and reference in the shared calendar. Mark the previous entry as superseded or remove the obsolete reminder. Tell everyone affected by the change, including transport helpers. Keep a short note of when the correction was verified.

## Example

If one message says Thursday and another says Friday, a fresh confirmation from reception should become the reference everyone uses for travel and reminders.
