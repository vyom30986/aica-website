## Short answer

Include an enquiry reference, time received, contact details, reason for the callback, assigned person and agreed callback window. Add an outcome field so attempts and completed conversations remain distinguishable.

## Practical explanation

Start with the minimum fields the team will actually maintain. Record the customer's preferred channel and any relevant contact restrictions. Use a specific owner for each callback. After an attempt, note whether contact was made and what the next action is. Review overdue callbacks at shift handover. Keep sensitive clinical details in the appropriate record rather than an unrestricted general task list.

## Example

“Called, no answer, retry at agreed time” describes a different state from “customer reached, collection details confirmed”. Both should remain visible in the tracker.
