## Short answer

Assign an owner to review phone numbers, opening hours, addresses and contact forms whenever operational details change. The website should reflect the same confirmed information that reception gives patients.

## Practical explanation

Keep a list of pages and external profiles where contact details appear. After a change, update the relevant sources and test the visible links. Call or verify the published number through the clinic's process. Check map destinations against the written branch address. Submit a clearly labelled test enquiry to verify delivery and remove or retain it according to the team's test procedure.

## Example

Changing a number in the footer may leave an old number on the contact page. A short inventory helps the owner update every relevant location.
