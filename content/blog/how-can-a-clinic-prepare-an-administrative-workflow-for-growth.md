## Short answer

Document how requests enter, who owns each stage and how completion is recorded before adding more channels or staff. Growth is easier to manage when the process has clear responsibilities and a visible queue.

## Practical explanation

Map the current journey from enquiry to outcome. Identify steps that depend on one person's memory or private inbox. Establish shared references, status definitions and handover rules. Test the process with a new staff member using synthetic examples. Review workload and delays using consistent measures. Add tools where they solve an observed problem, then verify that the revised process still works across shifts and branches.

## Example

If only one employee knows where pending requests are stored, the first improvement may be a shared queue and clear ownership before introducing another customer contact channel.
