## Short answer

Maintain a short log of the issue, date raised, support reference and next expected action. This helps you follow up coherently when different support staff respond over several days.

## Practical explanation

Use the official support channel and describe the affected account without sharing login secrets. Save confirmation emails and note any promised response window. Update the same entry when you receive a reply. Distinguish an issue being acknowledged from it being resolved. Before closing the request, test the original task and record whether it now works. Keep any remaining problem visible for the next response.

## Example

A password-reset email arriving may complete one step, while being able to sign in and open the correct account confirms the practical outcome.
