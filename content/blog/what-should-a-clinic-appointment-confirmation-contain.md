## Short answer

Include the confirmed date, time, provider or service, branch, booking reference and contact route for changes. Add only the preparation or document instructions that the clinic has actually approved for that appointment.

## Practical explanation

Use a consistent message template and populate it from the confirmed booking record. Check the recipient and avoid unnecessary private details in previews. Explain how to reschedule and whether any acknowledgement is requested. If the arrangement changes, issue a clear update identifying the new details. Keep a copy of the confirmation linked to the booking so staff can resolve questions consistently.

## Example

A confirmation that identifies both the branch and the service helps patients who have several appointments at different locations within the same clinic group.
