## Short answer

Ask how the booking will be confirmed, what documents to bring, where to attend and how to contact the clinic if plans change. Reception can explain the administrative process and route clinical questions to the appropriate professional.

## Practical explanation

Write down the appointment reference and confirm the spelling of your name and contact number. Ask whether forms should be completed beforehand and whether any accessibility support can be arranged. If the clinic gives preparation instructions, request them in a form you can revisit. Confirm who to contact when you do not understand an instruction rather than guessing what it means.

## Example

A first-visit checklist can cover confirmation, location, paperwork, accessibility and the cancellation or rescheduling contact.
