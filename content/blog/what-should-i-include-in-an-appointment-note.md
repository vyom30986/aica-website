## Short answer

Record the date, time, provider, location or joining link, booking reference and anything the clinic specifically asks you to bring. Add your own questions in a separate section so logistical details and discussion points are easy to find.

## Practical explanation

Copy the confirmed appointment details into one calendar entry. Include travel information if the clinic has multiple branches. Write the reception number beside the booking reference so you can contact the right team if plans change. When instructions are unclear, ask the clinic before the visit. Update the same entry after rescheduling instead of leaving several competing reminders.

## Example

An appointment entry that says “Tuesday, 10 am, second-floor clinic, reference 214” is easier to act on than a message that only says “doctor tomorrow”.
