## Short answer

Use one row per enquiry, a unique reference, consistent statuses and a clear next-action field. Protect the meaning of each column so sorting and filtering do not separate notes from the request they describe.

## Practical explanation

Freeze the header row and filter the whole record range. Keep phone numbers as text. Avoid merged cells inside the enquiry list. Use separate dates for creation and next follow-up. Assign an owner for correcting inconsistent entries and reviewing duplicates. Limit access according to staff responsibilities and follow the business's retention procedures. Periodically check that staff can find overdue and unassigned requests quickly.

## Example

A filter for “new, owner blank” can reveal requests that entered the sheet successfully but have not yet been assigned to anyone.
