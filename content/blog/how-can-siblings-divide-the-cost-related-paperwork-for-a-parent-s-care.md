## Short answer

Agree who collects invoices, who records payments and where the shared administrative documents will be stored. Keep the arrangement transparent and based on the parent's preferences and the family's actual responsibilities.

## Practical explanation

Use one record per invoice with its provider, date and payment status. Preserve original receipts and record reimbursements separately from the original payment. Share access only with the people handling the task. Resolve discrepancies using the provider's receipt or statement. When responsibility changes, reconcile outstanding items before handing over so the next person can see which paperwork is complete.

## Example

One sibling can collect receipts while another maintains the payment log. A common invoice reference links their work without requiring duplicate spreadsheets.
