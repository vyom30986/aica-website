## Short answer

Start by asking the person whose information it is what they want shared, with whom and for which purpose. Different relatives may need different levels of detail to help with a particular task.

## Practical explanation

Discuss concrete examples such as appointment reminders, transport arrangements and copies of reports. Use smaller sharing groups or direct messages where appropriate. Record the agreed contact preferences and revisit them when responsibilities change. Avoid treating participation in a family chat as blanket agreement to distribute documents. If a provider needs formal permission for someone to act on the person's behalf, follow that provider's process.

## Example

Someone helping with a ride may need the clinic address and time, while a person attending the consultation may need access to the requested paperwork.
