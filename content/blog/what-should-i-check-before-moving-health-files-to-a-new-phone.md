## Short answer

Verify that the files and accounts you need are accessible on the new device before relying on it for appointments. A successful phone transfer does not necessarily mean every cloud account, offline file or app session is ready.

## Practical explanation

List the storage locations you use and sign in through their normal processes. Open a sample of important documents and check multipage files. Confirm notification settings and appointment calendar entries. Keep the old device available until the necessary checks are complete, following its provider's guidance for secure transfer and removal. Avoid deleting the only usable copy of a document during cleanup.

## Example

Open the actual report needed for your next visit on the new phone, rather than assuming that seeing its filename proves the file is available.
