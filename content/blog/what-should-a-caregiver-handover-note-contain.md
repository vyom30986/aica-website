## Short answer

Include the tasks completed, tasks still open, upcoming appointments and where relevant documents are stored. A handover should give the next person enough context to continue practical support without having to reconstruct a whole conversation.

## Practical explanation

Use a date and the names of the people handing over. Distinguish confirmed information from questions awaiting a reply. Include the contact details for the provider involved and the person's preferences about assistance. Read the note together when possible, giving the next caregiver time to ask questions. Keep care instructions tied to the professional's written guidance rather than rewriting them from memory.

## Example

“Clinic called, Friday slot confirmed, report still pending” makes three separate states visible and gives the next caregiver a clear starting point.
