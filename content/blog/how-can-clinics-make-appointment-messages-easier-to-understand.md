## Short answer

Use plain language, a clear action and the details needed to carry it out. Put the date, time and location together, and explain any required reply in terms the patient is likely to recognise.

## Practical explanation

Review templates with reception staff and, where appropriate, users who can comment on clarity. Avoid unexplained abbreviations and competing instructions. Check that translated messages preserve dates and booking details accurately. Keep the clinic's contact route visible for questions. Test how the message appears on a phone, including whether important information is cut off in a preview. Update the template when feedback reveals confusion.

## Example

“Reply to confirm you can attend on Friday at 10 am” gives a clearer next step than “Kindly acknowledge accordingly” without a date or expected response.
