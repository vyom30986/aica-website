## Short answer

Saving means the document is stored and retrievable. Reviewing means the appropriate healthcare professional has considered it and explained the next step. Keeping those stages distinct helps prevent an administrative task from being mistaken for a completed consultation.

## Practical explanation

After downloading a report, check that it is complete and record when it arrived. Follow the provider's process for arranging a review. Write down the outcome of that conversation and any agreed follow-up arrangements. If you do not know whether a review has occurred, ask the clinic directly. Avoid using an app's upload confirmation as evidence of clinical review.

## Example

A tracker can use “downloaded”, “sent to clinic” and “review discussed” as separate milestones with their own dates.
