## Short answer

Record who is making the call, what they are asking and when a reply is expected. A visible “awaiting reply” status helps other caregivers coordinate before opening another conversation about the same request.

## Practical explanation

Agree on a primary contact for each appointment. After a call, add the outcome and any reference number. If the clinic asks for a callback, record the time or condition that triggers it. When another person takes over, hand over the existing request rather than starting again without context. Escalate through the clinic's process when a promised reply is overdue.

## Example

A shared note saying “Riya called at 11 am, reception will confirm by evening” gives others a clear reason to check the existing request before calling again.
