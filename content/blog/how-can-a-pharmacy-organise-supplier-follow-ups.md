## Short answer

Use a supplier task list with the order reference, question, responsible staff member and expected response. Link the supplier's answer to the original order so staff do not rely on an isolated message.

## Practical explanation

Record the date of the last contact and the next action. Keep confirmed delivery information distinct from estimates. When a supplier changes an arrangement, update the relevant customer-facing tasks as well. Review outstanding items at a predictable time each day. Preserve documents such as order acknowledgements in the appropriate business record rather than copying them into unrelated customer notes.

## Example

A supplier confirming a revised delivery date should trigger a review of any customer callback that depended on the earlier estimate.
