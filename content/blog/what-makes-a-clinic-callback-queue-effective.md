## Short answer

Every callback needs a reason, owner, contact route, agreed time window and outcome. The queue should show which calls are due, which have been attempted and which still require action.

## Practical explanation

Separate administrative callbacks from clinical questions and assign them accordingly. Record contact preferences and relevant availability. After each attempt, update the outcome and next step. Review unassigned items before the end of the shift. When another person takes over, preserve the original question and previous attempts. Keep the queue within the clinic's authorised record system and access arrangements.

## Example

A receptionist can return a call about opening hours, while a question about a care plan needs the designated clinical team and its normal response process.
