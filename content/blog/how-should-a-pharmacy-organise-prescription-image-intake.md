## Short answer

Keep the image linked to the correct customer request and place it in a queue for the authorised pharmacist's review. Staff should be able to see whether the document was received, is readable and has been reviewed.

## Practical explanation

Check that the upload belongs to the intended customer and includes the complete document. Ask for a clearer copy when it cannot be read. Preserve the original image and avoid editing its clinical content. Use distinct statuses for received and pharmacist-reviewed. Limit access to the staff who need it and follow the pharmacy's record-handling procedures. Record any request for clarification against the same enquiry.

## Example

An automated upload acknowledgement can confirm receipt while the internal queue still correctly shows that professional review is pending.
