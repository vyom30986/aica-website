## Short answer

Choose categories that determine who handles the request and what happens next. A small, clear set is usually easier for patients and staff to use than a long list of overlapping labels.

## Practical explanation

Review recent enquiries and group them by the team or workflow needed. Include an option for uncertain requests and assign someone to review those entries. Explain category boundaries to staff with examples. Avoid asking patients to make clinical judgements just to reach the right contact. Review the distribution after launch and adjust categories that repeatedly cause misrouting.

## Example

“Appointment change”, “document request” and “billing question” point to practical workflows. A vague category such as “important” does not identify the responsible team.
