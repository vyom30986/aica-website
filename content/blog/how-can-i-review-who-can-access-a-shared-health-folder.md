## Short answer

Use the storage service's sharing controls to inspect named members and link settings, then compare that access with the person's current wishes and support arrangements. Review both individual files and the folder when permissions can differ.

## Practical explanation

Identify people who no longer need access and use the normal controls to remove it. Check whether links are restricted to named recipients or usable more broadly. Confirm that remaining helpers can still access the documents they need. Ask for help from the service provider if the permission model is unclear. Repeat the review when caregiving roles change or a temporary helper finishes.

## Example

Removing someone from a group chat does not necessarily remove their access to a separately shared document folder. Check the folder's own settings.
