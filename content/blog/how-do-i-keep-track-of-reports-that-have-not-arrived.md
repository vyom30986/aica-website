## Short answer

Maintain a small list of expected reports with the provider, test or document name, expected availability and contact details. Mark a report as received only after you have opened the complete file.

## Practical explanation

Ask the provider how results will be delivered and whether you need to arrange a review. Record the collection or visit reference so staff can locate the request. If the promised time passes, contact the provider through its normal channel. Keep delivery status separate from clinical review status. Receiving an email or download link does not establish that the clinician has reviewed its contents.

## Example

Two separate fields, “received” and “review appointment arranged”, make the next administrative step easier to identify.
