## Short answer

Identify the specific missing information and ask for it once in a clear message. Keep the request open with an owner and next review time so it does not disappear between shifts.

## Practical explanation

Use a short checklist appropriate to the enquiry type. Ask only for information required to process that request and use the pharmacy's approved channel. Record what was requested and when. When a reply arrives, attach it to the existing enquiry and have the responsible person review it. If the request cannot proceed, explain the next step without marking it completed prematurely.

## Example

“Please confirm the collection branch” is a precise administrative request. It is more useful than asking the customer to resend every detail from the beginning.
