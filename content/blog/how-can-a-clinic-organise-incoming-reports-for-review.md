## Short answer

Create a receipt queue linked to the correct patient and appointment, with a separate status for professional review. Staff should be able to identify missing pages, mismatched details and unassigned documents before they are overlooked.

## Practical explanation

Record when the document arrived and where it is stored. Verify identifying details through the clinic's procedure. Route it to the authorised clinician or team and record acceptance where needed. Keep technical receipt separate from interpretation or approval. Track documents awaiting clarification and requests for replacement copies. Use the clinic's established escalation process for items that require prompt attention.

## Example

A document can be marked “received, awaiting clinician review” without implying that its contents have already been assessed.
