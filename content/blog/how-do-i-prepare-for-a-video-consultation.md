## Short answer

Confirm the joining method, appointment time and contact number, then test the device and connection before the appointment. Keep requested documents and your question list available so setup problems use as little consultation time as possible.

## Practical explanation

Choose a private space where you can hear clearly. Charge the device and test the microphone and camera. Ask the clinic how to share documents and what to do if the connection drops. Have the booking reference nearby. If someone will help you join or communicate, tell the clinic and agree on their role. The clinician will decide whether an in-person assessment is needed.

## Example

A five-minute rehearsal with the joining instructions can reveal an expired link or muted microphone before the appointment begins.
