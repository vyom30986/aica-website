## Short answer

Use calendar labels that clearly identify the appointment owner while respecting what should appear on a shared screen. Agree who will receive confirmations and who is responsible for acting on them.

## Practical explanation

Check which phone number the clinic has recorded for each person. Avoid assuming that the person holding the device knows every booking. Keep a simple private appointment list with names, dates and references. Review notification previews with the phone owner and decide how sensitive information should appear. When responsibility changes, update the clinic's contact details through its normal process.

## Example

A shared calendar reminder can identify the person and appointment time while a private note holds the detailed documents and questions.
