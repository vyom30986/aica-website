## Short answer

Use a short handover focused on open bookings, promised callbacks, unresolved document issues and changes affecting the next shift. Each item should identify the next action and the staff member responsible for it.

## Practical explanation

Maintain the handover information during the shift rather than reconstructing it at closing. Record confirmations and uncertainties separately. Highlight changes to provider availability or branch arrangements that affect patient messages. Let the incoming team ask questions and confirm ownership. Keep sensitive details in the appropriate record and use references in the general handover where possible.

## Example

“Booking 214 needs a callback after the revised schedule is approved” gives the next shift a dependency and a concrete action.
