## Short answer

Ask customers how they prefer to receive updates and record that preference with the relevant contact details. Recheck it when a customer reports a change or when the recorded channel is no longer working.

## Practical explanation

Provide a simple way to correct phone numbers and email addresses through the pharmacy's normal process. Confirm the intended recipient when a number is shared by a household. Keep service updates and optional promotional preferences distinguishable. Apply a requested change wherever the contact is used. Record the change date so staff can understand why an older message used different details.

## Example

A customer may want collection updates by phone but prefer email for invoices. Recording both purposes avoids treating one preference as universal.
