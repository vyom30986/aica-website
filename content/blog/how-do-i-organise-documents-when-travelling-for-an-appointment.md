## Short answer

Keep the booking, travel details and requested records together while leaving a recoverable copy in your usual storage location. A travel folder should make the immediate visit manageable without becoming the only copy of important documents.

## Practical explanation

Confirm the clinic address and allow for its check-in process. Download required files and check that they open. Carry originals if requested and use a folder that keeps pages together. Record whom to contact if transport delays your arrival. After returning, add the new consultation paperwork to your normal archive so the travel folder does not become a separate, forgotten record system.

## Example

A return-trip checklist can include the new summary, receipts, follow-up booking and any documents still expected from the provider.
