## Short answer

Use a small number of folders or labels for appointments, documents and administrative queries, and keep a clear way to identify messages that still need action. Organisation should make the next step visible rather than merely clearing the inbox.

## Practical explanation

File completed conversations after saving important attachments in your normal record system. Flag messages awaiting a reply and add a reminder when the provider gives a deadline. Keep booking references in the calendar entry. Verify the sender through a known channel if a message asks for unusual information or payment. Review old action flags periodically so completed requests do not remain outstanding.

## Example

An “Awaiting clinic reply” label can hold a rescheduling request until the new slot is confirmed, after which the calendar becomes the main reference.
