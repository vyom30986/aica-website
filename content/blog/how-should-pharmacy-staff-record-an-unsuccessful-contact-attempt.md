## Short answer

Record the time, channel, outcome and agreed next step without treating the enquiry as resolved. Use factual wording that describes what happened rather than making assumptions about the customer's intentions.

## Practical explanation

Choose a small set of outcomes such as no answer, message delivered or contact details need confirmation. Follow the customer's preferences and the pharmacy's contact process when planning another attempt. Keep repeated attempts linked to the same enquiry. If the matter needs professional attention, route it appropriately. Review whether the next attempt has an owner and due time.

## Example

“Called at 3 pm, no answer, retry tomorrow morning as previously agreed” is clearer than “customer unavailable” with no indication of what happens next.
