## Short answer

Provide the clinic's approved instructions in a consistent, retrievable format and give patients a clear route for questions. Reception should avoid improvising preparation guidance when a clinician or service protocol must determine it.

## Practical explanation

Identify which appointment types have specific instructions and who maintains them. Include the relevant approved version with the booking confirmation. Use clear language and check that links or attachments open correctly. Route uncertainty to the appropriate professional. Record when instructions were sent and any clarification provided. Review templates when the service changes so old wording is not reused automatically.

## Example

If a patient asks whether an instruction applies to their appointment, reception can identify the booking and obtain an authorised answer rather than guessing.
