## Short answer

Record each visit date, provider, reason for attending in your own words and the location of the consultation summary. Keep the timeline brief so it helps you locate evidence rather than duplicating the entire medical record.

## Practical explanation

Add an entry soon after each appointment while the practical details are clear. Quote instructions only when you can check them against the clinician's written record. Mark uncertainty explicitly and ask the clinic to clarify it. Link to the original summary or report instead of rewriting lengthy passages. Bring the timeline when it helps explain the sequence of previous visits.

## Example

A one-line entry can say “12 November, follow-up visit, summary saved in Clinic folder” and point to the full document when more detail is needed.
