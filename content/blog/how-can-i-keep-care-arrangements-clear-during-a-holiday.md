## Short answer

Review provider opening times, planned appointments and the availability of family helpers before the holiday begins. Put confirmed arrangements into the shared plan so last-minute assumptions are less likely to cause confusion.

## Practical explanation

Contact providers about administrative schedules that affect existing bookings. Check whether usual transport or collection arrangements will be available. Assign responsibility for any pending document or callback. Keep the current contact list accessible and distinguish routine contacts from services intended for urgent help. Update the plan after a cancellation or delay, including any reminder that still points to the old arrangement.

## Example

If a report desk closes early, note the confirmed collection window and the person collecting it rather than leaving the task as “pick up this week”.
