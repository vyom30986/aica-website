## Short answer

Tell customers when routine enquiries are reviewed and place incoming requests in a queue for the next staffed period. Keep the acknowledgement accurate about what has and has not happened.

## Practical explanation

Configure an appropriate message with opening hours and the normal contact process. Make sure staff review the overnight queue at opening and assign each request. Keep duplicate messages together when they concern the same enquiry. Avoid implying that an automated reply means a pharmacist has reviewed the request. Follow the pharmacy's established guidance for messages that require a different level of response.

## Example

An acknowledgement can say that the request was received and will be reviewed during opening hours, while the internal queue retains its “new” status.
