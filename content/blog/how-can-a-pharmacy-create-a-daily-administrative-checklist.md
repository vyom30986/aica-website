## Short answer

List the routine tasks that need a clear owner and completion record, then group them by when they happen. Keep the checklist short enough to be used during a normal working day.

## Practical explanation

Ask staff which tasks are most often missed at opening, handover and closing. Include concrete actions such as reviewing unanswered enquiries and reconciling promised callbacks. Assign ownership and define what completion means. Keep clinical and regulated procedures within the pharmacy's established professional processes. Review the checklist after a week of use and remove items that duplicate another reliable control.

## Example

“Review callbacks due today and assign remaining items” is a verifiable task. “Keep customers happy” is an aspiration that cannot be checked off meaningfully.
