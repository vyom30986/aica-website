## Short answer

Agree who owns each task and record its current status in one shared place. A named owner makes it easier to distinguish an intention to help from a booking, collection or call that has actually been completed.

## Practical explanation

List upcoming tasks with the person's agreement, then assign one responsible caregiver and a backup where useful. Include due dates and the information needed to act. Record completion with a short factual note. When plans change, update the shared list before notifying others. Keep private clinical details in the appropriate record rather than copying everything into a broad family chat.

## Example

One person can own booking the appointment while another owns transport. Each task needs its own confirmation so neither person assumes the other has handled both.
