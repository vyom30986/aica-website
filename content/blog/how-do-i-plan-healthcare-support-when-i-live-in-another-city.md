## Short answer

Map out the practical tasks that can be handled remotely and the ones requiring someone nearby. Agree on a local contact and a shared communication plan with the person receiving support.

## Practical explanation

Keep provider contact details and upcoming appointments organised. Ask how the person wants you involved in calls or document sharing. Identify who can help with travel, collecting paperwork or using a device when needed. Record confirmed arrangements rather than relying on informal promises. Review the plan after changes in location, availability or preference so the contact list stays useful.

## Example

A remote family member may arrange a booking while a nearby relative accompanies the visit. Both should know how the appointment outcome will be recorded and shared.
