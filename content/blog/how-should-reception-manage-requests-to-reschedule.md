## Short answer

Link the rescheduling request to the existing booking and confirm the replacement arrangement before updating all related reminders. Keep the patient informed about whether a new slot is confirmed or still being discussed.

## Practical explanation

Verify the booking reference using the clinic's usual identity checks. Record the patient's preferred alternatives and route clinical timing questions to the appropriate professional. Once a replacement is agreed, update the appointment record, reminders and any related transport or document arrangements the clinic manages. Preserve an understandable change history. Confirm the final details with the patient through the agreed channel.

## Example

An old reminder should not remain active after a new appointment is confirmed. Check both the booking record and the message schedule.
