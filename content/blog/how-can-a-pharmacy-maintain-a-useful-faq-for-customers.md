## Short answer

Base the FAQ on repeated administrative questions and keep each answer tied to an actual service or policy. Assign someone to review changes so customers receive the same information online and at the counter.

## Practical explanation

Start with opening hours, location, collection arrangements and contact methods. Ask staff which questions repeatedly interrupt the same workflow. Write concise answers and identify the owner who can confirm each one. Route medical questions to the pharmacist rather than turning the FAQ into personalised treatment advice. Record the review date and update the FAQ whenever the underlying service changes.

## Example

If collection hours differ from general opening hours, explain both clearly and ensure the counter team gives the same information.
