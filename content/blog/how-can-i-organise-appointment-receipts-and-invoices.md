## Short answer

Store each receipt with its visit date, provider and payment reference, and keep it separate from the clinical report when practical. This makes administrative questions easier to resolve without searching through unrelated medical documents.

## Practical explanation

Save the original invoice or receipt as supplied. Check the patient name, date and amount and ask the provider about any discrepancy. Note whether a payment is pending, completed or being queried. If you need documents for reimbursement, ask the relevant organisation which records it requires. Avoid relying on a payment screenshot alone when a formal invoice is requested.

## Example

A visit folder can contain a “Documents” section and a “Receipts” section, both labelled with the same appointment reference.
