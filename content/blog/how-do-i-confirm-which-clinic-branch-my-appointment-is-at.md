## Short answer

Check the full address on the booking confirmation and contact reception if the branch is unclear. A provider's name alone may not identify the building, floor or entrance you need.

## Practical explanation

Save the confirmed branch address with the appointment reference. Compare the map destination with the written address before starting your journey. Ask about the correct entrance and any access arrangements you need. If the appointment changes, verify whether the branch changes too. Share the final location with the person travelling with you so everyone uses the same information.

## Example

A clinic may operate at two locations with similar names. Recording the street and branch alongside the appointment time helps avoid a wasted journey.
