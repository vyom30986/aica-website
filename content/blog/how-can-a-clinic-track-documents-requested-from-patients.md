## Short answer

Use a checklist linked to the booking that states which document is needed, why the request was made, when it was requested and whether it has arrived. Keep the wording specific enough for patients to act on.

## Practical explanation

Ask only for documents required by the clinic's process or the clinician's request. Explain the accepted format and delivery channel. Record partial receipt, unreadable files and missing pages accurately. When the patient responds, update the existing request instead of opening a duplicate task. Check whether the receiving team needs to review the document before the appointment can proceed.

## Example

“Previous visit summary, requested on Monday, received Wednesday” creates a clearer history than a generic “paperwork pending” note.
