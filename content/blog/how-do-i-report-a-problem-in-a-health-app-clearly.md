## Short answer

Describe the task you were attempting, the steps taken, the result you expected and what actually happened. Include the app version and device details when available, while keeping unnecessary personal information out of the report.

## Practical explanation

Try to identify the exact screen and time of the issue. Capture an error message as written. If a screenshot helps, remove unrelated private information before sharing it through the provider's support channel. Explain whether the problem happens every time or only occasionally. Keep the support reference and record any workaround the provider recommends. Do not send passwords or one-time login codes.

## Example

“The report upload stops after I select a PDF on Android” gives support a starting point that “the app is broken” does not provide.
