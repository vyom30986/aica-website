## Short answer

Record the promise as a task with a due time, responsible person and specific information to be confirmed. A promise in a chat message is easy to overlook unless it becomes part of the team's working queue.

## Practical explanation

At the end of each customer conversation, identify any commitment made by staff. Add it to the callback or task list and link the original enquiry. Review tasks before their due time. If the information remains unavailable, contact the customer with an accurate status update through the agreed channel. Record the new expectation and avoid repeating an unconfirmed deadline.

## Example

“Update customer after supplier confirms delivery” needs an owner who will check the supplier response and then complete the customer update.
