## Short answer

Define each status with an entry condition and the next expected action. Staff should understand what evidence allows a request to move forward, rather than choosing labels based on personal interpretation.

## Practical explanation

Write a short status guide and practise with realistic synthetic enquiries. Discuss ambiguous cases during training. For each status, specify who owns the request and whether a due date is needed. Review a small sample of live records after rollout to find inconsistencies. Adjust the definitions when the workflow changes and communicate the change to all shifts.

## Example

“Awaiting customer” should mean a specific question has been sent and a reply is needed, with the question and date recorded in the enquiry.
