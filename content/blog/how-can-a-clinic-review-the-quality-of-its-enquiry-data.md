## Short answer

Check a sample of records for complete contact details, consistent categories, assigned owners and usable next actions. Data quality is about whether staff can carry out the work, as well as whether fields contain values.

## Practical explanation

Define the fields required for each enquiry type. Look for placeholders, duplicate requests and statuses that contradict the notes. Correct records through the clinic's approved process and identify why the error occurred. Improve form labels or staff guidance when the same issue repeats. Keep the sample and review criteria consistent enough to compare later results. Avoid treating every optional blank as an error.

## Example

A phone field containing “call later” is populated but unusable. A separate availability note and a verified contact field would support the task more clearly.
