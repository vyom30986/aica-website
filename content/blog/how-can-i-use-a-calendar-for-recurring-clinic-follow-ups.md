## Short answer

Enter the follow-up arrangement given by the clinic and record whether the actual booking is confirmed. A calendar can remind you to arrange a visit as well as remind you to attend one, but those are different tasks.

## Practical explanation

If the clinician advises a review window, ask reception how and when to book within it. Create a booking task with the clinic's contact details. Once a slot is confirmed, add the precise appointment time and remove the booking task. Update the calendar after every review because the next arrangement may change. Share reminders only with the people helping with that appointment.

## Example

“Call clinic to book review” belongs before “Attend confirmed review”. Keeping both stages prevents a reminder from implying that a slot already exists.
