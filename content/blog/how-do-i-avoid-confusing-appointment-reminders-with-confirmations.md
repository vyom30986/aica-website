## Short answer

Treat a booking as confirmed when the provider's process says it is confirmed. A personal calendar reminder or an automated acknowledgement may only show that a request was submitted.

## Practical explanation

Read the wording of the message and look for the date, time, provider and booking reference. If a message says someone will contact you, record the request as pending. Keep the final confirmation in the appointment entry when it arrives. Before travelling, resolve any conflicting times with the clinic. If you reschedule, remove or update the old reminder to avoid acting on it later.

## Example

“We received your request” and “Your appointment is confirmed for Friday” describe different stages and should have different statuses in your own notes.
