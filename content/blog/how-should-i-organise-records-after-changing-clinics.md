## Short answer

Prepare a concise document bundle relevant to the new appointment and keep the original archive intact. Ask the receiving clinic what it needs so you can transfer useful information in an accepted format.

## Practical explanation

Gather referral documents, previous summaries and the reports requested by the new provider. Label each file by its original date and source. Record any documents you have requested but not received. Confirm delivery with the receiving clinic and bring a backup if requested. When the new team clarifies a discrepancy, save the clarification with the document instead of silently altering the original record.

## Example

A transfer checklist can show which items were requested, which were sent and which still need to be obtained from the previous clinic.
