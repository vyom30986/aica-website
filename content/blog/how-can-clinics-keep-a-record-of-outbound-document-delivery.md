## Short answer

Record what was sent, when, to whom and through which approved channel, with a reference to the original document. Delivery tracking should remain distinct from whether the recipient has read or understood the contents.

## Practical explanation

Verify the recipient using the clinic's process before sending. Check the document version and page count. Record a delivery failure or access problem and assign follow-up. If the recipient requests another copy, use the same record to preserve the history. Avoid copying sensitive contents into a broad communication log when a document reference is sufficient. Follow the clinic's retention and access procedures.

## Example

A message marked delivered may still lead to a call saying the attachment cannot be opened. Record that issue and the action taken to provide an accessible copy.
