## Short answer

Ask about the practical details that make appointments easier, such as preferred language, communication support, transport and who should accompany them. Record the preferences in their own terms and confirm them before making arrangements.

## Practical explanation

Choose a short list rather than assuming one preference applies to every visit. Contact the clinic early about support it can provide. Keep personal preferences separate from confirmed availability. Recheck the arrangements when booking a new provider or branch. Give the person an opportunity to change their mind, especially about who attends or receives information.

## Example

A note might say “prefers Hindi explanations and wants a family member present for paperwork”, followed by whether the clinic has confirmed those arrangements.
