## Short answer

Create one folder for the visit, with the appointment details, the questions you want answered and the documents the clinic has requested. Put recent material first so you can find it while speaking with the clinician.

## Practical explanation

Start with the booking confirmation and make a short list of what you need to bring. Add the relevant prescription, reports and previous consultation notes. Keep the original documents available when the clinic requests them, even if you also have photographs. Check that every page belongs to the correct person. Before leaving, open the folder on your phone and locate each item without using the search bar.

## Example

A folder called “November clinic visit” can hold the confirmation and three relevant reports. Older records can stay in a separate archive that remains available if requested.
