## Short answer

Compare each task's due date with its current status and owner, then review overdue items for the action needed. Keep tasks waiting on another party visible with their dependency recorded.

## Practical explanation

Define how due dates are set and who can change them. Review unassigned tasks as well as overdue ones. When a deadline moves, record the reason and any patient update required. Avoid clearing an overdue flag by marking a task complete before its outcome is achieved. Look for recurring bottlenecks and address the underlying workflow. Keep clinical escalation governed by the clinic's established procedures.

## Example

A report request awaiting a provider response may need a reminder, while a task with no owner needs assignment before another deadline is useful.
