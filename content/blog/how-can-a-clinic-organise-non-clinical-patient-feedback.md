## Short answer

Collect the specific experience, relevant date or service and a contact preference if a response is wanted. Route feedback to a responsible person and distinguish an observation from a formal complaint or clinical concern.

## Practical explanation

Use categories that help assign action, such as directions, booking communication or reception access. Preserve the person's wording when summarising. Avoid collecting unrelated health information in a general feedback form. Record the response and any process change. Review repeated themes over time rather than treating each submission as isolated. Route clinical or safety concerns through the clinic's established professional procedures.

## Example

Several comments about finding the entrance may justify updating the confirmation message with clearer directions and checking whether later feedback improves.
