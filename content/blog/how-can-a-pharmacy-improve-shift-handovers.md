## Short answer

Review open customer requests, pending collections, supplier follow-ups and tasks needing pharmacist attention before the next shift takes over. Every outstanding item should have a current status and a named owner.

## Practical explanation

Use a consistent handover list and update it during the shift. Separate routine administrative tasks from issues requiring professional review. Highlight promised callbacks and any time-sensitive arrangements already agreed with customers. Confirm that the incoming staff member understands the next action. Record the handover time and avoid relying on information that exists only in a departing employee's private messages.

## Example

An open collection enquiry should show the customer reference, last confirmed information and the staff member responsible for the next update.
