## Short answer

Convert each agreed administrative next step into a task with an owner and due date, linked to the visit. Staff should work from the documented outcome rather than infer the plan from a brief conversation.

## Practical explanation

Identify actions such as arranging a review, requesting a document or sending an approved summary. Keep clinical decisions with the authorised professional. Record dependencies so a task waiting for approval is visible. Review open tasks daily and confirm completion with an outcome note. If a plan changes, update the affected tasks and messages. Avoid leaving old reminders active after the change.

## Example

“Send summary after clinician approval” has two stages. Recording approval and sending separately helps staff see exactly where the task is waiting.
