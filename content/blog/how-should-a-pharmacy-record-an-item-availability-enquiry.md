## Short answer

Record the exact item requested and the status of the availability check, with a clear distinction between an enquiry and a confirmed order. Ask for clarification when the request does not identify the item sufficiently.

## Practical explanation

Have the appropriate staff member verify the requested product details against the available stock information. Record who checked and when, because availability can change. Tell the customer what has been confirmed and what remains pending. Avoid treating an unverified enquiry as a reservation. Route any question about substitutions or prescription interpretation to the pharmacist through the normal process.

## Example

A status such as “availability checked at 2 pm, customer response pending” is more informative than a single “available” flag with no time or owner.
