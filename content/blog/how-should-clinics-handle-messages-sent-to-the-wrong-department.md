## Short answer

Route the request to the appropriate team using the clinic's established process and tell the sender what to expect. Keep a record of the handover so the request does not vanish after being forwarded.

## Practical explanation

Identify the nature of the request without collecting unnecessary details. Use an internal reference and assign an owner for the transfer. Confirm whether the receiving team has accepted responsibility. Tell the patient if they need to contact a different channel themselves. Avoid promising a response time that the receiving department has not agreed. Follow the clinic's process for urgent or sensitive messages.

## Example

A billing query sent to appointments can be transferred with its reference and contact details, while the original record notes which team now owns the response.
