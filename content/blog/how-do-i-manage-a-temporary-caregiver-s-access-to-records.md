## Short answer

Share the information needed for the agreed period and tasks, and plan how access will end when the arrangement finishes. Explain the folder structure and contact process so the temporary helper can work confidently.

## Practical explanation

Confirm the person's agreement and the helper's responsibilities. Use individual access where the tool supports it rather than distributing a shared account password. Show how to find relevant appointments and documents. At handover, review unfinished tasks and remove access that is no longer needed through the tool's normal controls. Check whether temporary downloads need to be returned or removed.

## Example

A relative helping for one week may need access to that week's appointment documents without receiving a permanent copy of the family's entire archive.
