## Short answer

Use a short agenda focused on upcoming appointments, missing documents and practical tasks. Ask the person receiving support which topics they want discussed and who should participate.

## Practical explanation

Start with changes since the last check-in. Review open tasks and confirm whether the assigned person can still complete them. Record new actions with an owner and date, then close tasks that have been completed. Keep discussion notes factual and share only the agreed details. If a question requires a healthcare professional, record who will contact the provider rather than trying to settle it through a family vote.

## Example

A ten-minute conversation can cover next week's visit, transport and an outstanding report without repeating the entire history of previous appointments.
