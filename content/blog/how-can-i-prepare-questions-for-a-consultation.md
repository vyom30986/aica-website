## Short answer

Write down the main concern and the decisions you need help with, then put your most important question first. A short list gives you a reference when the conversation moves quickly or you feel distracted.

## Practical explanation

Use your own words and include what you have noticed, when it started and what you want clarified. Leave space to record the clinician's explanation. Ask for unfamiliar terms to be explained and confirm what should happen next. If several issues need discussion, tell the clinician at the start so you can agree on priorities. Keep questions that remain unanswered for the follow-up.

## Example

Instead of “discuss everything”, a practical note might say “understand this report, confirm the follow-up plan, and ask whom to contact with questions”.
