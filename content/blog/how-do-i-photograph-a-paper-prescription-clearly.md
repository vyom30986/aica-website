## Short answer

Place the page flat in good light, keep the camera parallel to it and capture the whole document. The aim is a readable copy that preserves the original wording, date and page edges.

## Practical explanation

Avoid glare, shadows and filters that alter the writing. Take another photograph if any text is blurred. Open the result and zoom in before sending it. Include additional pages in their original order and avoid cropping away instructions. If handwriting remains unclear, ask the issuing clinic or pharmacist to clarify it rather than trying to reconstruct missing words. Keep the paper original in its folder.

## Example

A clear image of the entire page is more useful than several close-ups with no indication of how the pieces fit together.
