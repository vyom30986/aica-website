## Short answer

Compare the document date, provider, page count and any revision label before deciding that two files are duplicates. Similar filenames can represent different versions, additional pages or a corrected report.

## Practical explanation

Open both files and check their identifiers. Keep the original provider-issued document and label a corrected version clearly if one exists. When you are uncertain, ask the provider which version should be used. Avoid editing results inside a downloaded report to make it match another copy. Once you have confirmed the authoritative version, organise copies so the right file is easy to select.

## Example

“Report” and “Report final” may be different documents. The contents and provider's revision information are more reliable than the filename alone.
