## Short answer

Look for the service's upload confirmation and verify that the correct document can be opened from the intended record. A progress bar reaching the end may be only one stage of processing.

## Practical explanation

Check the patient or profile selected before uploading. After completion, inspect the filename, date and page count. If the service shows a pending or failed status, follow its instructions before trying again. Avoid repeated submissions without checking whether a previous attempt created a copy. When a clinic is the recipient, use its process to confirm receipt if that matters for the appointment.

## Example

A file appearing under the wrong family profile is still an upload problem even if the application displays a successful transfer message.
